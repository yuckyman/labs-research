# Abstract 7160: NEK2 drives pathogenesis, drug resistance, and LMP1 expression in EBV-positive non-Hodgkin lymphoma

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>Non-Hodgkin lymphoma (NHL) is one of the most common cancers worldwide, representing 90% of malignant lymphomas. NHL is a diverse group of malignancies, and a subset of these lymphomas are caused by infection with the human gammaherpesvirus, Epstein-Barr virus (EBV). Finally, using a humanized mouse model of EBV-driven lymphomagenesis, we demonstrate that NEK2 inhibition significantly decreased tumor burden and tumor incidence while prolonging survival in vivo. Taken together, our data suggest NEK2 inhibition as a promising treatment strategy for EBV-positive NHL.</jats:p>
