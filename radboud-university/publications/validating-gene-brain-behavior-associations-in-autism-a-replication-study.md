# Validating gene-brain-behavior associations in autism: A replication study.

## Abstract

Autism is characterized by heterogeneity in clinical characteristics, neuroimaging and genetic profiles. To elucidate the multi-level heterogeneity, Buch et al. (2023) identified biologically meaningful subgroups in Autism Brain Imaging Data Exchange (ABIDE). However, ABIDE contains non-biological sources of variability due to retrospectively aligned site protocols. To separate biological from site-related variability and test the robustness of these subgroups, we assessed how well their findings replicate in a deeply phenotyped, prospectively collected autism dataset (EU-AIMS LEAP).
