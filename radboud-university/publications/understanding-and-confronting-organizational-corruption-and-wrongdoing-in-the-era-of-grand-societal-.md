# Understanding and Confronting Organizational Corruption and Wrongdoing in the Era of Grand Societal Challenges: Past, Present, and Future Perspectives

## Abstract

<jats:p>Organizational corruption and wrongdoing remain persistent problems in contemporary life and are closely linked to the grand societal challenges humanity faces. This Special Issue, therefore, asks: How are organizational corruption and wrongdoing linked to grand societal challenges? We posit that corruption and wrongdoing are dynamic forces that interact reciprocally with grand societal challenges. In an inside-out dynamic, organizational corruption and wrongdoing hinder, distort, or reverse progress on grand societal challenges. In an outside-in dynamic, grand societal challenges and broader societal transformations reconfigure the incentives, opportunities, pressures, justifications, and visibility conditions through which corruption and wrongdoing emerge. This editorial traces the evolution of corruption research from classic economic and public-sector accounts toward a business-and-society perspective and develops a dynamic framework of the corruption–grand societal challenges nexus. We analyze how the Special Issue articles advance this conversation and conclude with a research and practice agenda for scholars and practitioners.</jats:p>
