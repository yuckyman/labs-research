# Hypersomnolentie na een traumatisch hersenletsel

## Abstract

<jats:p>Hypersomnolence after traumatic brain injury

Symptoms of hypersomnolence are is estimated to occur in 27 to 47% of patients suffering a traumatic brain injury (TBI). This can induce stress in short-, but also long-term. However, these symptoms are often underestimated by the patient, and/or inadequately diagnosed and treated by clinicians. In this narrative review, we hope to highlight the importance of routine examination and provide guidance in the differential diagnosis and treatment of hypersomnolence after TBI.</jats:p>
