# POLÍTICAS PÚBLICAS FEDERAIS DE FINANCIAMENTO DO ESPORTE PARALÍMPICO NO BRASIL:  EXPLORANDO O CASO DO RUGBY EM CADEIRA DE RODAS

## Abstract

<jats:p>The development of parasport in Brazil has progressed with incentives from public incentives, policies, and fostering programs that structure access to high-performance sport. This study aimed to examine the flow of financial resources made available through the main federal public policies that fund Paralympic sport—namely the Agnelo/Piva Law, the Sports Incentive Law (LIE), and the Athlete Grant Program—and how these resources reach Wheelchair Rugby (WR). This is a qualitative, descriptive, and exploratory investigation based on documentary analysis of institutional reports, legislation, and publicly available data covering the period from 2021 to 2025. The findings indicate an increase in Agnelo/Piva transfers to the Brazilian Paralympic Committee and the Brazilian Wheelchair Rugby Association, reflected in the amounts allocated to the WR. The Brazilian Committee of Parasport Clubs emerges as a complementary actor, particularly in providing operational support to affiliated clubs. We observed a strong concentration of LIE resource acquisition among a small number of clubs, revealing inequalities in access and limitations in project development and fundraising capacity. We also identified a relatively small number of Athlete Grants awarded to WR compared to other Paralympic sports, suggesting disparities in the distribution of this benefit. Although federal funding mechanisms have contributed to the structuring of the WR, challenges related to equity, transparency, and the institutional capacity of clubs remain. These findings highlight the need for policies that broaden access to financial resources and mitigate inequalities in the development of Wheelchair Rugby in Brazil.
