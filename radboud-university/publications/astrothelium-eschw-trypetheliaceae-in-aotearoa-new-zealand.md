# Astrothelium Eschw. (Trypetheliaceae) in Aotearoa / New Zealand

## Abstract

<jats:p>The presence of Astrothelium nigratum (Müll.Arg.) Aptroot &amp; Lücking (Trypetheliaceae) in the lichenised mycobiota of Aotearoa / New Zealand is documented. Furthermore, the lichen described as Trypethelium bicolor var. pyrenuloides C.Knight in 1884 and treated as Trypethelium phlyctaena Fée in the 1985 edition, as Trypethelium variolosum Ach. [≡ Astrothelium variolosum (Ach.) Müll.Arg.] in the 2007 edition of the Flora of New Zealand lichens (Galloway 2007), and in 2016 as Astrothelium phlyctaena (Fée) Aptroot &amp; Lücking, is here treated as a heterotypic synonym of Astrothelium nigratum. This action removes Astrothelium / Trypethelium phlyctaena and Astrothelium / Trypethelium variolosum from the lichenised mycobiota of Aotearoa / New Zealand. Astrothelium nigratum is a widespread, common, though under-collected, lichen of mature forest known from a range of locations in Te Ika-a-Māui / North Island and from at least one location in the northwestern portion of Te Waipounamu / South Island. We assess Astrothelium nigratum as ‘Not Threatened’ using the New Zealand Threat Classification System.</jats:p>
