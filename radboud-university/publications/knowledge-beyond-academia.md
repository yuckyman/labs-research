# Knowledge beyond academia?

## Abstract

<jats:p>The academic work of theologians rests on types of knowledge not always explicit in their texts. In this article, I analyse an interview with the late South African theologian Klaus Nürnberger to highlight these types of knowledge. By contrasting the contribution of the interview with the knowledge available in Nürnberger’s published works, I show how the interview adds to the knowledge base surrounding Nürnberger’s academic work. I find that the value found in the interview was: (i) limited though still useful clarifications of his academic work; (ii) capturing some of Nürnberger’s orality; (iii) imparted wisdom from costly life choices; (iv) intensely personal background and motivation to his work as a philosopher and theologian; and (v) as a training ground for young theologians.</jats:p>
