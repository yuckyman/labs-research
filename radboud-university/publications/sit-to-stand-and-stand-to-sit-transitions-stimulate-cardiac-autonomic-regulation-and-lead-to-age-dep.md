# Sit-to-stand and stand-to-sit transitions stimulate cardiac autonomic regulation and lead to age-dependent short-term patterns in heart rate

## Abstract

<jats:sec>

                    <jats:p>Sedentary behaviors pose health risks, which can be mitigated by regular exercise. The impact of everyday movements on cardiac autonomic regulation is not well-established.
