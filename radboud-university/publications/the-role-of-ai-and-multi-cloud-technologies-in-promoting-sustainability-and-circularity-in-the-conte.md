# The Role of AI and Multi-Cloud Technologies in Promoting Sustainability and Circularity in the Context of Modern Manufacturing

## Abstract

<jats:p>This chapter explores the role of artificial intelligence (AI) and multicloud architectures in advancing sustainability within the framework of modern manufacturing. By combining digital scalability, computational elasticity, and energy efficiency, these technologies emerge as strategic enablers to reduce carbon emissions, optimize resources, and foster practices aligned with the circular economy. Key dimensions such as energy efficiency, intelligent workload location, sustainable elasticity, and support for IT circularity are examined, highlighting both benefits and challenges. Beyond theoretical and bibliometric analysis, expert interviews reveal how the convergence of AI and multicloud can transform business models, strengthen digital environmental governance, and enhance competitiveness. The findings indicate that aligning technological innovation with social and environmental responsibility provides a promising path to consolidate modern manufacturing as a driver of shared prosperity and environmental regeneration.</jats:p>
