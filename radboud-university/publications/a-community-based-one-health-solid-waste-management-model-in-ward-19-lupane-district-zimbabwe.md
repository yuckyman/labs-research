# A Community-Based One Health Solid Waste Management Model in Ward 19, Lupane District, Zimbabwe

## Abstract

This case study documents the development and implementation of a community-based One Health solid waste management model in Ward 19 of Lupane District, Zimbabwe. The plastic was sold at USD 0.12 per kilogram, generating approximately USD 113.28 during the first recycling cycle. Equally important, the process established a community-led Solid Waste Management Committee, trained 46 community members, strengthened collaboration among institutions, and empowered local communities to take an active role in managing waste. This case demonstrates the value of applying One Health principles to address complex environmental challenges in rural settings. It provides practical evidence that community-led and transdisciplinary approaches can generate environmental,
