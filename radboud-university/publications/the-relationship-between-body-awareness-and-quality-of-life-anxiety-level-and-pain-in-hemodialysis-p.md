# The relationship between body awareness and quality of life, anxiety level and pain in hemodialysis patients

## Abstract

<jats:p xml:lang="en">Hemodialysis (HD) patients often experience high symptom burden, impaired quality of life, and psychological distress. Body awareness, defined as the perceived ability to respond to bodily sensations, has been linked to pain perception, emotional regulation, and overall functioning. However, its role in HD populations remains unclear. The present study investigated the associations of body awareness with quality of life, anxiety, and pain in patients receiving maintenance HD.
