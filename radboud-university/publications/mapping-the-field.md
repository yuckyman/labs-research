# Mapping the Field

## Abstract

<jats:p>The Aotearoa Futures Network Map was built on a simple but powerful premise: that futures thinking is stronger when practitioners can see each other, connect across difference, and act in coordination. But a map is only as useful as the conversations it generates — and the futures it helps us collectively re-weave. In the spirit of Whiria te Anamata – TheGreat Re-Weaving, this participatory workshop invites futures practitioners, community connectors, and curious newcomers at Aotearoa Futures Forum 2026 to take an honest look at the Network Map.
About the speakersJade Tang-Taylor is a Designer, Facilitator, Innovator, Collaborator &amp; Educator, and deeply passionate about Co-Designing Better Futures. Currently, Jade wears a few hats alongside Design Futures Aotearoa; Industry Advisor at academyEX, Associate of ThinkPlace and Co-Found of simpact AI.
Howard Lange blends curiosity with strategic thinking to developinnovative solutions to complex challenges. He is drawn to projects involving strategic design and interconnected systems, embracing open conversation and shared ideas.
Victoria Mulligan is a builder of things - teams, strategies, services and futures - and uses strategic foresight and participatory futures tools to create more resilient and adaptive strategies, and purposeful cultures. She is the creator of the Aotearoa Futures Network Map, Co-Founder a Director of Design Futures Aotearoa. She is a member of the Global Futures Society and a regular contributor to consumer and academic publications worldwide.Note: Jade will be presenting this workshop on behalf of all three speakers.</jats:p>
