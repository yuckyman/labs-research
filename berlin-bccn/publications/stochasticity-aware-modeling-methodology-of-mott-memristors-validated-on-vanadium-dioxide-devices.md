# Stochasticity-Aware Modeling Methodology of Mott Memristors Validated on Vanadium Dioxide Devices.

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>
                    Volatile memristors based on Mott insulators, such as vanadium dioxide (VO
                    <jats:sub>2</jats:sub>
                    ), are envisaged as key components for neuromorphic computing e.g. to implement the Hodgkin-Huxley model. A deterministic electro-thermal model known as the Picket-Williams (PW) model is adapted to our micro-fabricated VO
                    <jats:sub>2</jats:sub>
                    -based memristors. The tuned model accurately captures the experimental memristive behaviors of our device for different operating temperatures (35 and 45 °C). The transitions voltages and current of the reversible metal-insulator transitions are notably well reproduced. We further establish the mathematical relations between the fluctuations of the intrinsic material parameters and the cycle-to-cycle variations of the switching voltages. The such-obtained stochastic propagation model is then applied to feed a Monte-Carlo simulation on the tuned PW-model, demonstrated capable of reproducing the experimental device fluctuation statistics (with an error smaller than 0.5 mV on the standard deviation). The proposed data-driven methodology is general and could be applied to other models accounting for memristors stochasticity, paving the way for faithful statistical modelling of neuromorphic circuits.
                  </jats:p>
