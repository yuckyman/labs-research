# Populism over partisanship on TikTok: the German case of Sahra Wagenknecht

## Abstract

<jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Introduction</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>TikTok's significance for political expression has steadily increased in recent years. Previous research on the platform has primarily focused on its use by political actors, with a smaller sample size. In this study, the emphasis shifts away from institutional actors toward user behavior.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
                  <jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Methods</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>By studying their online interactions with political accounts on the platform, we ask: “What do users prioritize: populism or policy alignment?” The study used a novel TikTok dataset containing 833,410 comments posted in response to 442 German political accounts up until November 2023.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
                  <jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Results</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>Studying the German political sphere, with a particular focus on the left-wing populist politician Sahra Wagenknecht and her then newly founded party, we find that users frequently engaging with the populist far-right party interact with Sahra Wagenknecht's content more than users interested in the remaining parties. This engagement is primarily driven by positive interactions.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
                  <jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Discussion</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>Our results offer further insight into the complex interplay between populism and partisan alignment and reinforce the potential explanatory value of a populist axis in classifying political systems.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
