# Foundations to Futures in Learning Engineering

## Abstract

<jats:p>The Journal of Learning Engineering (JoLE) is fortunate to have had the support of an accomplished advisory board whose members have helped shape the field of learning engineering from multiple disciplinary, research, and professional vantage points. For the inaugural issue, the editorial team invited these esteemed board members to participate in an asynchronous roundtable, reflecting on the field’s foundations, its current state, and developments that may shape its future. Some responses have been lightly edited and, where necessary, condensed for clarity and length, while making sure to preserve each contributor’s unique perspectives and voice. Together, these responses offer areas of convergence and productive differences in how the field’s development, challenges, and opportunities are experienced from a variety of contexts.  </jats:p>
