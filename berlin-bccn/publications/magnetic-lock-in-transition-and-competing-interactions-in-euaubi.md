# Magnetic lock-in transition and competing interactions in EuAuBi

## Abstract

<jats:p>
                    The zero-field magnetic ground state of the superconducting polar semimetal
                    <a:math xmlns:a="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
                      <a:mi>EuAuBi</a:mi>
                    </a:math>
                    is determined using neutron powder diffraction. The system realizes a commensurate antiferromagnetic ground state of wave vector
                    <b:math xmlns:b="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
                      <b:mrow>
                        <b:mi mathvariant="bold">k</b:mi>
                        <b:mo>=</b:mo>
                        <b:mo>(</b:mo>
                        <b:mn>1</b:mn>
                        <b:mo>/</b:mo>
                        <b:mn>3</b:mn>
                        <b:mo>,</b:mo>
                        <b:mn>0</b:mn>
                        <b:mo>,</b:mo>
                        <b:mn>0</b:mn>
                        <b:mo>)</b:mo>
                      </b:mrow>
                    </b:math>
                    with moments along the crystallographic
                    <d:math xmlns:d="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
                      <d:mi>b</d:mi>
                    </d:math>
                    axis and unequal moment amplitudes between the two magnetically inequivalent Eu orbits. On warming above an intermediate transition temperature,
                    <e:math xmlns:e="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
                      <e:mrow>
                        <e:msub>
                          <e:mi>T</e:mi>
                          <e:mrow>
                            <e:mi mathvariant="normal">N</e:mi>
                            <e:mn>2</e:mn>
                          </e:mrow>
                        </e:msub>
                        <e:mo>≈</e:mo>
                        <e:mn>3.3</e:mn>
                        <e:mspace width="0.16em"/>
                        <e:mi mathvariant="normal">K</e:mi>
                      </e:mrow>
                    </e:math>
                    , a commensurate-incommensurate transition occurs with
                    <i:math xmlns:i="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
                      <i:mrow>
                        <i:mi mathvariant="bold">k</i:mi>
                        <i:mo>(</i:mo>

                      </i:mrow>
                    </i:math>
                    , preserving the moment direction and occurring only within a narrow temperature window below the incommensurate-paramagnetic transition at
                    <k:math xmlns:k="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
                      <k:mrow>
                        <k:msub>
                          <k:mi>T</k:mi>
                          <k:mrow>
                            <k:mi mathvariant="normal">N</k:mi>
                            <k:mn>1</k:mn>
                          </k:mrow>
                        </k:msub>
                        <k:mo>≈</k:mo>
