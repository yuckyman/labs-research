# Profiling care to parents and co-residence patterns in Europe: inequalities across regions

## Abstract

<jats:p>Co-residence with care-dependent older adults has been recognised as a crucial factor influencing the well-being of carers. In this descriptive article, we identify less favourable patterns among co-residential carers regarding socio-demographic, economic and housing-related aspects and health, well-being and social resources and explore heterogeneities in these inequalities across European regions using data from Wave 9 of the Survey of Health, Ageing and Retirement in Europe. The results show large regional differences in the prevalence of co-residence with dependent parents, as well as in inequalities between different groups of extra- and co-residential carers. Notably, carers in familialistic Southern and Eastern care regimes report higher care intensity.</jats:p>
