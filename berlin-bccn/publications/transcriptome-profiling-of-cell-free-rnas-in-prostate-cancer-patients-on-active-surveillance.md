# Transcriptome profiling of cell-free RNAs in prostate cancer patients on active surveillance

## Abstract

<jats:title>Summary</jats:title>
                <jats:p>In this feasibility study, plasma cfRNA transcriptomes from 64 men with prostate cancer on active surveillance revealed differences between stable disease and progression to radical prostatectomy and suggested a predominantly non-tumour origin of circulating RNA. These findings demonstrate the feasibility of cfRNA transcriptomic analysis in this setting and support its further investigation as a minimally invasive adjunct for personalised risk stratification and monitoring.</jats:p>
