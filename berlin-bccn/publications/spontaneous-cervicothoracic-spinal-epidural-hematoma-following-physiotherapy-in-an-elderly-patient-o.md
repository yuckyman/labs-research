# Spontaneous cervicothoracic spinal epidural hematoma following physiotherapy in an elderly patient on apixaban

## Abstract

<jats:sec id="st1">
                    <jats:title>Background:</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>Spontaneous spinal epidural hematomas (SSEHs) are rarely reported in patients on direct oral anticoagulants (DOACs), such as apixaban. Here, we report an 87 years old who developed a SSEH following cervical physical therapy.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
                  <jats:sec id="st2">
                    <jats:title>Case Description:</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>An 87-year-old male on apixaban presented with acute neck pain and rapidly progressive quadriparesis following manual cervical physiotherapy. The magnetic resonance imaging revealed an extensive dorsal epidural hematoma extending from C1 to T8, causing severe cord compression. After anticoagulation reversal, urgent multilevel partial hemilaminectomy and hematoma evacuation were performed. The patient fully recovered neurological function 1 year later.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
                  <jats:sec id="st3">
                    <jats:title>Conclusion:</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>Extensive cervicothoracic SSEH may occur in patients on DOAC following cervical physical therapy.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
