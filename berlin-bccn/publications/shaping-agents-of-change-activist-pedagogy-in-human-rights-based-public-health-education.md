# Shaping Agents of Change: Activist Pedagogy in Human Rights-Based Public Health Education

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>This practice note focuses on activist pedagogy in human rights-based public health education. Activist pedagogy, situated in critical applied pedagogy, can prepare students and learners to challenge the systems and structures of power that diminish and violate human rights. Educators can prepare learners with the skills necessary to incorporate activist approaches into their professional practice. The author shares an approach, reflective podcasting, which aims to facilitate internalization of information learned in the course through the development of tacit knowledge that can form the foundation for the application of activist approaches to protect and promote human rights through public health practice.</jats:p>
