# The Influence of Environmental Factors on Sleep During Recovery from Acquired Brain Injury: A Systematic Review

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Objective</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>To investigate the influence of environmental factors on sleep in children and adults recovering from acquired brain injury (ABI).</jats:p>

                    <jats:title>Results</jats:title>
