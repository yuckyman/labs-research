# Evaluating an In Person Depolarization Program and Testing a Light Touch Enhancement

## Abstract

<p>We evaluated if an intensive intervention involving in-person, multi-hour conversations reduced animosity and improved empathy. We also experimentally tested if stacking a brief video intervention highlighting sympathetic personal narratives on the intensive intervention boosts the ability of the intensive intervention to reduce animosity and increase empathy for people with different opinions. Across events (N = 209, Events k = 10), we found that people reported less animosity and more empathy following the events. In our preregistered pre-test post-test experimental design, we found the video intervention may improve liking and respect for people with different opinions, but the tests were not typically significantly different from zero. In summary, although the program improves intergroup relations, we were not able to significantly boost the effects of the intensive treatment with a light touch video treatment. Suggestions for future research are discussed.</p>
