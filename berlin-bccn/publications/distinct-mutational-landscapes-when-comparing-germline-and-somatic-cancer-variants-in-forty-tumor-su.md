# Distinct mutational landscapes when comparing germline and somatic cancer variants in forty tumor suppressor genes.

## Abstract

<jats:p>
                  Germline and somatic cancer variants in tumor suppressor genes (TSGs) share loss of function mechanisms with studies of a few genes (
                  <jats:italic>DICER1</jats:italic>
                  ,
                  <jats:italic>CEBPA</jats:italic>

We obtained 32,941 P/LP germline and 12,907 O/LO somatic variants. Only 3,863 (9.2%) variants were shared. Eighteen TSGs showed significant differences in distributions of variant occurrences by consequence replicated with non-overlapping somatic data from the COSMIC database (chi-squared tests, false discovery rate=5%).
                  <jats:italic>DICER1</jats:italic>
                  ,
                  <jats:italic>TP53</jats:italic>
                  , and
                  <jats:italic>SMAD4</jats:italic>
                  displayed excess somatic missense events, while 9 TSGs (e.g.,
                  <jats:italic>RB1</jats:italic>
                  ,
                  <jats:italic>APC</jats:italic>

Characterizing these distinctions enables more accurate clinical interpretation of cancer variants.
