# Brasinoesteroides: estructura, biosíntesis y función en la regulación del crecimiento y la adaptación de las plantas

## Abstract

<jats:p> 
Plant hormones are substances that are found in small quantities in plant organs and these in turn fulfill functions in the cells that in reaction generate morphological modifications ranging from growth to the generation of new individuals, since they regulate vital functions, development and cell multiplication, which is why the study of their function and biosynthesis in plant tissues causes concern, since in addition to being found within the plant, it has been found the way to generate them synthetically in order to understand the effects they cause. Among these phytohormones are gibberellins, auxins, cytokinins, ethylene, jasmonic acid, abscisic acid and a new class called brassinosteroids, each of which generates a different effect, which is why studies and trials have been conducted, where the combination of these substances can generate significant physiological changes.</jats:p>
