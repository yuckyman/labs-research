# O CONFLITO COMO DISPOSITIVO PEDAGÓGICO: POTENCIALIZANDO DIVERGÊNCIAS PARA DESENVOLVIMENTO DE COMPETÊNCIAS SOCIOEMOCIONAIS

## Abstract

<jats:p>This article proposes a reflection on the diversity present in the school environment and the complexities of interpersonal interactions that emerge in this scenario. Often, the plurality of voices and identities result in divergences that, if neglected, can transform into conflicts and challenges to the teaching-learning process. However, this research argues that such differences, when not silenced and properly mediated, constitute a valuable and favorable pedagogical resource for the development of the socio-emotional competences recommended by the National Common Curricular Base (BNCC). Through a literature review, the study highlights conflict as a strategic tool for promoting socio-emotional skills and attitudes. The discussion is based on the theoretical contributions of Candau (2000, 2012) and Esteban (2001, 2013) regarding conflicts generated by diversity, as well as on the approaches to teaching socio-emotional skills by Vinha (2017). Thus, it is believed that fostering divergence in the classroom is essential for building a democratic school and for the formation of a subject who recognizes and values differences.
