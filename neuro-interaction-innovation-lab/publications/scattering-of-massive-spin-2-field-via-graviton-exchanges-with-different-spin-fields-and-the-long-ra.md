# Scattering of massive spin-2 field via graviton exchanges with different spin fields and the long range gravitational potential

## Abstract

<jats:p>
                    In this work, we compute the graviton mediated scattering amplitude of a massive spin-2 Fierz-Pauli field with various other massive spin fields, and in the nonrelativistic limit, find out the corresponding two-body gravitational potentials. The massive spin-2 field does not represent gravity here. The theory of gravity is taken to be the usual massless general relativity, and the massive spin-2 field is taken as a test quantum field coupled to gravity via the standard minimal prescription. We first compute the tree level 2-2 scattering of a massive spin-2 field with massive scalar, spin-1, and spin-
