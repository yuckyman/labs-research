# Cross-Functional Collaboration

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>Cross-functional collaboration is essential for developing effective population health policies. This chapter emphasizes the importance of involving diverse stakeholders—such as quality and safety, ethics, care coordination, diversity, population health management, behavioral health, human resources, patient experience, and legal departments—in the development, implementation, and evaluation of these policies. Building on the foundational principle of cross-functional collaboration, the chapter explores how the unique contributions of each stakeholder shape the content, scope, and effectiveness of policies. By fostering collaboration across functions, healthcare organizations can create comprehensive, inclusive strategies that advance population health and health equity, ultimately improving outcomes and well-being across populations.</jats:p>
