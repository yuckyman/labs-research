# &lt;b&gt;PSICOEDUCAÇÃO E ORIENTAÇÃO PARENTAL E DESENVOLVIMENTO DAS FUNÇÕES EXECUTIVAS EM ESTUDANTES COM TDAH&lt;/b&gt;

## Abstract

<jats:p>Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) is a neurodevelopmental disorder that, in addition to inattention, hyperactivity, and impulsivity, involves impairments in executive functions, impacting school performance, routine organization, and family relationships. Recent literature highlights the role of the family as a privileged context for strengthening these skills, especially when parents and caregivers receive clear information and structured support about the disorder. In this scenario, parental psychoeducation emerges as a promising resource by articulating knowledge about ADHD, behavioral management, and self-regulation strategies in daily life. This article presents a theoretical review of national and international publications from 2020 to 2025, located in databases such as PubMed, SciELO, Scopus, and Google Scholar, that address psychoeducational interventions aimed at families of children and adolescents with ADHD and their effects on executive functions. In general, studies indicate that parental psychoeducation programs promote greater understanding of the disorder, greater consistency in parenting practices, better organization of routines, and improvements in components such as sustained attention, planning, and self-control. These findings reinforce the importance of including the family in multimodal intervention proposals and point to the need for further research that considers different sociocultural and educational contexts.</jats:p>
