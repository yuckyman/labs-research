# Synthesis and Action

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>This final chapter emphasizes the crucial role of intentionally and purpose in shaping population health policy, underscoring an emphasis on inclusion and belonging. In healthcare, policy must transcend the technicalities of insurance and financing to address systemic inequities and ensure that no individual or group is left behind. By integrating the 10 foundational principles of population health policy, health professionals can create a safer, more inclusive, and more effective healthcare system. This chapter provides a practical action framework, reviewing tips and action items to guide the application of these principles, offering a comprehensive framework for transforming population health policy into a dynamic, equitable approach that truly serves all populations.</jats:p>
