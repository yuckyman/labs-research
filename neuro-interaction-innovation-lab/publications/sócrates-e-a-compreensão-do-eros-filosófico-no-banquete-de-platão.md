# SÓCRATES E A COMPREENSÃO DO EROS FILOSÓFICO NO “BANQUETE” DE PLATÃO

## Abstract

<jats:p>This article pretends to analyze the original meaning of the concept of philosopher and philosophy, dating back to the beginnings of the development of philosophical activity. Considering Socrates as the first to systematically develop philosophy as a discipline in the West, and Plato as the main source for understanding the Socratic doctrines; this study is more specifically aimed at clarifying of these concepts from the Platonic-Socratic perspective, observing the close relationship between the way Plato understands them and the figure of Socrates in the dialogues. Among the many Platonic works, the “Banquet” stands out, not only for the theme it addresses, but also due to the special way in which it portrays Socrates' actions</jats:p>
