# Digital Integration in Literacy Instruction: Proficiency and Utilization of Digital Game-Based Learning Strategies Among Elementary Teachers

## Abstract

<jats:p>In response to the growing demand for digital integration in education, this study investigates how elementary teachers in the Talacogon West District employ digital tools to enhance literacy instruction. Using a descriptive-correlational design, data were collected from 121 respondents across 16 primary schools. The study employed mean, percentage, and analysis of variance (ANOVA) to examine teachers’ proficiency and the extent of digital strategy utilization. Results revealed that teachers’ proficiency in digital game-based learning strategies was at a moderate level, with similarly moderate implementation of instructional goals and utilization of digital instructional strategies. ANOVA results further indicated significant differences in proficiency and implementation levels across selected teacher demographics. These findings suggest that while digital tools are increasingly adopted in literacy instruction, their integration remains at a developmental stage. The study underscores the importance of sustained professional development and institutional support to strengthen teachers’ digital proficiency and advance the effective use of instructional technologies in elementary education.</jats:p>
