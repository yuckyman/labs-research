# The Sphere Packing Problem in Dimension Seven

## Abstract

<jats:p>We prove that the supremum of sphere-packing densities in $\mathbb{R}^7$ is $\pi^3/105$, achieved by the $E_7$ root lattice. The proof is local: every Voronoi cell in every unit-ball packing satisfies $\vol(V_c)\ge16$, with equality only at $\sqrt{2}\,E_7$. Shell localisation, support-function containment, and radial monotonicity reduce the problem to $115$ chamber types enumerated by the Weyl group $W(E_7)$; for each type a free-volume identity $\vol(V_0)=16+F_\Omega$ reduces the bound to $F_\Omega\ge0$. This is proved at Level~A by a rational $LDL^\top$ decomposition of the $7\times7$ radial Hessian (minimum pivot $823/3360$, exact over $\mathbb{Q}$), and at Level~B by a monotonicity argument: the support function $h_B(u_1(\theta))$ of the $125$-halfspace body is non-increasing from $2$ to $1$ as $\theta$ runs from $0$ to $\pi/2$, so $F_\Omega(\theta)$ is non decreasing and inherits positivity from the Level~A bound at $\theta=0.10$. Level~A covers $[0,0.10]$ exactly; the monotonicity argument covers $[0.10,\pi/2)$. Both parts are unconditional.</jats:p>
