# Evaluation of the Research Writing Readiness of High School Educators: Basis for the Development of a Research Capability Enhancement Program

## Abstract

<jats:p>This study evaluated the research writing readiness of high school educators and identified the hindrances they encounter in conducting research, serving as the basis for the development of a Research Capability Enhancement Program (RCEP). Guided by the Vitae Researcher Development Framework (RDF), the study utilized a sequential explanatory mixed-methods design involving 126 teachers for the quantitative phase and nine participants for the qualitative phase through focus group discussions. Descriptive statistics and thematic analysis were employed to analyze the data. Findings revealed that teachers demonstrated moderate to high readiness in terms of knowledge base, cognitive abilities, and professional conduct, particularly in foundational research concepts and ethical practices. However, difficulties were evident in higher-order skills such as data analysis, interpretation, and academic writing. Moreover, teachers experienced significant hindrances in research writing, including time constraints, workload pressure, emotional fatigue, limited collaboration, and insufficient access to resources and funding. Qualitative results supported these findings, highlighting gaps between teachers’ awareness of research and their actual engagement in research activities. Based on the results, a structured three-year Research Capability Enhancement Program (RCEP) was proposed to address identified gaps and challenges. The program emphasizes capacity building, mentoring, collaboration, and institutional support mechanisms to enhance teachers’ research competencies. The study concludes that while teachers are generally research-aware, targeted and sustained interventions are necessary to strengthen their research productivity and foster a sustainable research culture in educational institutions.</jats:p>
