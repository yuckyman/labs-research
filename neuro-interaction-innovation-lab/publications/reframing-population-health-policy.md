# Reframing Population Health Policy

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>This chapter reframes population health policy and addresses the urgent need for innovative approaches, particularly within healthcare systems, where the complexity of policy development intersects with the imperative to enhance team dynamics, cross-functional collaboration, and patient care. The chapter introduces 10 foundational steps, which will be expanded upon in subsequent chapters with their corresponding foundational principles, which guide the development of purposeful population health policy. An overview of the practical deliverables include model strategies, policies, and action plans aligned with national accreditation and benchmarking standards. This new framework for policy development and analysis bridges the gap between theory and practice.</jats:p>
