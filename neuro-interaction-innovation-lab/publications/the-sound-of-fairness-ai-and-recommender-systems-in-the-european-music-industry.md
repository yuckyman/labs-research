# The sound of fairness: AI and recommender systems in the European music industry

## Abstract

<jats:p>Recommender systems (RS) play a central role in structuring visibility in music platforms. In this context, fairness has emerged as a key normative concept guiding the discussions in the music governance. However, there is a gap in understanding the meaning of fairness in the light of RS. This article adds to this gap by examining how European music professionals conceptualize fairness in relation to RS and Artificial Intelligence (AI). Drawing on 22 semi-structured interviews and a survey of 360 respondents, the study adopts a qualitative grounded approach informed by insights from discursive institutionalism. The findings show that transparency emerges as the central dimension of fairness. Opacity is perceived as reinforcing platform power, shaping visibility and monetization, and impacting cultural diversity. AI is viewed as intensifying these dynamics, through opaque training practices and the promotion of AI-generated content. While there is broad support for strengthening transparency, there is less consensus regarding how to promote cultural diversity. By foregrounding professional perspectives, the study conceptualizes fairness as a negotiated and context-dependent construct, contributing to a socially grounded understanding of algorithmic governance in digital music platforms.</jats:p>
