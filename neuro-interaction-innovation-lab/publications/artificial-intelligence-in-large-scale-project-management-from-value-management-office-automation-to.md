# Artificial Intelligence in large-scale project management: from value management office automation to strategic stewardship

## Abstract

<jats:p>Large-scale project delivery has always been hard. Data volumes grow, stakeholder networks sprawl across time zones, and the margin for error narrows as program budgets rise. What has changed in the past decade is that artificial intelligence (AI) now offers project organizations a credible set of tools to manage that complexity, not just by automating paperwork, but by reshaping how governance itself is structured. This article examines that reshaping, focusing on the organizational transition from the traditional Project Management Office (PMO) to the emerging Value Management Office (VMO). It is offered as a conceptual, practitioner-informed analysis rather than an empirical study: drawing together peer-reviewed literature and applied delivery experience, it analyzes how Machine Learning (ML) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) are reported to improve estimation accuracy, risk detection, and testing efficiency, and how those gains create new space for human leadership to operate at a more strategic level. Some implementation studies have reported deep learning models reaching 85–90% accuracy in project cost estimation (Hassan et al., 2025), while systematic reviews of machine-learning-based test selection and prioritization point to substantial reductions in manual regression effort (Pan et al., 2022). The article argues that these efficiency gains do not reduce the value of human judgment, they redirect it toward the decisions that matter most.</jats:p>
