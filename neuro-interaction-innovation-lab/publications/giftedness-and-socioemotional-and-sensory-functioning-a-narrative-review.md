# Giftedness and socioemotional and sensory functioning: a narrative review

## Abstract

<jats:p>Giftedness has historically been associated with both high cognitive performance and assumed emotional and social vulnerabilities. This narrative literature review aims to investigate, from a non-dysfunctional neurodivergence perspective, whether gifted individuals exhibit greater emotional dysregulation, sensory sensitivity, and socialization difficulties. Drawing on empirical studies with population-based samples and recent systematic reviews, results indicate no robust evidence that gifted individuals show a higher prevalence of emotional dysregulation, social difficulties, or sensory sensitivity compared to their peers. On the contrary, many studies suggest similar or even superior functioning across several socioemotional domains. It is concluded that negative associations frequently stem from methodological biases, stereotypes, and contextual variables.</jats:p>
