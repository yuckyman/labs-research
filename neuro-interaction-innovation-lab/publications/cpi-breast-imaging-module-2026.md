# CPI Breast Imaging Module 2026

## Abstract

Earn 10 CME with CPI Breast Imaging Module 2026, the latest module from the Continuous Professional Improvement program. This new breast imaging module contains 50 image‑rich questions across mammography, ultrasound, MRI, tomosynthesis, and CEM, covering benign and malignant pathology, normal variants, artifacts, and postoperative findings. BI‑RADS® v2025 updates applied throughout – with streamlined terminology and lexicon changes; Practical guidance for workup and management – including biopsy indications and radiologic‑pathologic concordance; Coverage of challenging scenarios – as well as insight into rare entities for advanced learners.
