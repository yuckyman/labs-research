# A New Method for Estimating Inertia and Frequency Nadir Dynamics in Microgrids

## Abstract

<jats:p>Coal plant replacement with renewable energy sources reduces grid inertia, making frequency drops more severe during disturbances. Predicting inertia, frequency nadir (fnadir) and its time of occurrence (tnadir) in real time is a useful parameter for device control frequency maintenance strategies. This paper proposes a novel Artificial Neural Network (ANN)-based model to predict inertia, fnadir, and tnadir in real time. The ANN model was trained, tested, and validated using data from a distribution system with a 50 ms sampling rate. The trained model, when integrated to the microgrid, is activated when the Rate of Change of Frequency (RoCoF) exceeds 0.04 Hz/s or the absolute frequency error surpasses 0.04 Hz. The predictions of the proposed model were compared with those of the classical method. It was found that the proposed ANN model performs better with a minimum error of 0.46%, an inference time under 100 ms, and an average error less than 1.3%. The model was validated on a modified IEEE standard distribution system with conventional generators and renewable energy sources (solar PV) in MATLAB/Simulink.</jats:p>
