# The Sphere Packing Problem in Dimension 4

## Abstract

<jats:p>We prove $\Delta_4 = \pi^2/16$: the optimal sphere packing density in $\mathbb{R}^4$ is achieved uniquely, up to isometry, by the scaled root lattice $\sqrt{2}\,D_4$.</jats:p>
