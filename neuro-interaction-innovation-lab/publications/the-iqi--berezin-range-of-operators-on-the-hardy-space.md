# The
                    <i>q</i>
                    -Berezin Range of Operators on the Hardy Space

## Abstract

<jats:p>This article investigates the geometric structure of the q-Berezin range of operators on the Hardy space, with a particular emphasis on convexity. Convexity results are established for several operator classes, including Toeplitz, weighted shift, and certain composition operators. The study demonstrates the relationship between the q-Berezin range and the classical Berezin range.</jats:p>
