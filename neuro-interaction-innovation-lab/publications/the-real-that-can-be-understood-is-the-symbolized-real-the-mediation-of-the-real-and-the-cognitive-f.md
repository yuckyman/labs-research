# THE REAL THAT CAN BE UNDERSTOOD IS THE SYMBOLIZED REAL THE MEDIATION OF THE REAL AND THE COGNITIVE FUNCTION OF LANGUAGE

## Abstract

<jats:p>The main objective of this article is to present some reflections on the human need to symbolize reality and the role of language in mediating this process of symbolization. Reality is total, complex, and very difficult for consciousness to grasp; therefore, in order for it to be known (analyzed, manipulated—that is, subjected to the scrutiny of reason), it must be symbolized through language. Symbolized reality creates an intersubjective reality, which is the very mediation of human knowledge. In this sense, the symbolization of the real—and imagination itself—is a stage of knowledge. Examples of this include the earliest rock inscriptions made in caves by primitive humans, myths and legends, maps and nautical charts drawn up by early navigators, mathematical theorems, poems, and novels, etc.</jats:p>
