# Rigidity of CMC Spacelike Hypersurfaces in a Lorentzian Product Space Under a Dirichlet Integral Condition

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>
                    We apply Bochner’s technique combined with a mild Dirichlet integral condition to show that a complete constant mean curvature (CMC) spacelike hypersurface immersed with positive semi-definite modified second fundamental form in a Lorentzian product space
                    <jats:inline-formula>
                      <jats:alternatives>
                        <jats:tex-math>$$\mathbb {R}_1\times M^n$$</jats:tex-math>
                        <mml:math xmlns:mml="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
                          <mml:mrow>
                            <mml:msub>
                              <mml:mi>R</mml:mi>
                              <mml:mn>1</mml:mn>
                            </mml:msub>
                            <mml:mo>×</mml:mo>
                            <mml:msup>
                              <mml:mi>M</mml:mi>
                              <mml:mi>n</mml:mi>
                            </mml:msup>
                          </mml:mrow>
                        </mml:math>
                      </jats:alternatives>
                    </jats:inline-formula>
                    , whose Riemannian base
                    <jats:inline-formula>
                      <jats:alternatives>
                        <jats:tex-math>$$M^n$$</jats:tex-math>
                        <mml:math xmlns:mml="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
                          <mml:msup>
                            <mml:mi>M</mml:mi>
                            <mml:mi>n</mml:mi>
                          </mml:msup>
                        </mml:math>
                      </jats:alternatives>
                    </jats:inline-formula>
                    has nonnegative sectional curvature, must be a slice
                    <jats:inline-formula>
                      <jats:alternatives>
                        <jats:tex-math>$$\{t\}\times M^n$$</jats:tex-math>
                        <mml:math xmlns:mml="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
                          <mml:mrow>
                            <mml:mrow>
                              <mml:mo>{</mml:mo>
                              <mml:mi>t</mml:mi>
                              <mml:mo>}</mml:mo>
                            </mml:mrow>
                            <mml:mo>×</mml:mo>
                            <mml:msup>
                              <mml:mi>M</mml:mi>
                              <mml:mi>n</mml:mi>
                            </mml:msup>
                          </mml:mrow>
                        </mml:math>
                      </jats:alternatives>
                    </jats:inline-formula>
                    . In particular, we conclude that the spacelike hyperplanes of the Lorentz–Minkowski space
                    <jats:inline-formula>
                      <jats:alternatives>
                        <jats:tex-math>$$\mathbb {L}_1^{n+1}$$</jats:tex-math>
                        <mml:math xmlns:mml="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
                          <mml:msubsup>
                            <mml:mi>L</mml:mi>
                            <mml:mn>1</mml:mn>
                            <mml:mrow>
                              <mml:mi>n</mml:mi>
                              <mml:mo>+</mml:mo>
                              <mml:mn>1</mml:mn>
                            </mml:mrow>
                          </mml:msubsup>
                        </mml:math>
                      </jats:alternatives>
                    </jats:inline-formula>
                    are the only complete CMC spacelike hypersurfaces immersed in
                    <jats:inline-formula>
                      <jats:alternatives>
                        <jats:tex-math>$$\mathbb {L}_1^{n+1}$$</jats:tex-math>
                        <mml:math xmlns:mml="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML">
                          <mml:msubsup>
                            <mml:mi>L</mml:mi>
                            <mml:mn>1</mml:mn>
                            <mml:mrow>
                              <mml:mi>n</mml:mi>
                              <mml:mo>+</mml:mo>
                              <mml:mn>1</mml:mn>
                            </mml:mrow>
                          </mml:msubsup>
                        </mml:math>
                      </jats:alternatives>
                    </jats:inline-formula>
                    , whose modified second fundamental form is positive semi-definite and the height function with respect to a spacelike hyperplane satisfies such a Dirichlet integral condition. A version of the classical Calabi–Bernstein Theorem to the context of Lorentzian product spaces is also given.
                  </jats:p>
