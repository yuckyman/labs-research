# LINGUISTIC ATLAS OF BRAZIL: PATHWAYS OF BRAZILIAN PORTUGUESE

## Abstract

<jats:p>This article presents an up-to-date perspective on the importance of a linguistic atlas, highlighting the *Atlas Linguístico do Brasil* (ALiB) and its contribution to revealing the country's linguistic reality regarding Brazilian Portuguese (BP). Through a vast sample of linguistic phenomena drawn from a corpus of approximately 3,600 hours of continuous speech by Brazilians from all five regions, the ALiB project essentially seeks to describe, analyze, interpret, and document the spoken language used by over 211 million Brazilians across the national territory. The informants in the sample are evenly distributed by gender and two age groups (18–30 and 50–65 years old); in state capitals, they are also distributed by two levels of education (elementary/middle school and university), all selected according to the criteria of multidimensional dialectology or geosociolinguistics. This paper outlines the results of three studies conducted within the scope of ALiB: one phonetic (palatalization of /l/ before /i/), one morphosyntactic (variation in the use of the definite article before personal names), and one lexical (terms for blush). The results demonstrate the strong influence of diatopic factors (capitals and regions) in conditioning the variants of these linguistic variables.</jats:p>
