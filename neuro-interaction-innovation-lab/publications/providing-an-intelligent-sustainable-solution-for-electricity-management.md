# Providing an Intelligent Sustainable Solution for Electricity Management

## Abstract

<jats:p>The climbing in population displacement in urban sectors has accelerated the adaptation of smart city policies across developing regions. Utilization of clean and sustainable energy is of utmost importance such as the demands of the entities can be met without any resource constraints. The management of energy in terms of electricity is therefore crucial to human livelihood. However, the present management process has flaws, leads to inefficiencies, and fraudulent activities, which further result in economic loss and resource wastage. To address such issues, this chapter proposes an intelligent Internet of Things (IoT) framework that uses tools like microcontrollers and sensors to minimize the need for human involvement, enhancing the system’s transparency and reliability. With such a consumer-friendly and secure system, live meter readings, automatic power cuts, and the detection of power theft, accidental situations can be performed effectively. The absence of wiring not only reduces installation costs but also minimizes the likelihood of faults. The integration of technologies such as IoT and artificial intelligence (AI) further improves reliability and consumer service. This proposed system can be a real game-changer that minimizes the involvement of intermediaries, promoting transparency and streamlining electricity management.</jats:p>
