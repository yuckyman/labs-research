# Trust

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>This chapter explores the foundational principle of securing trust, framed within the principles of high reliability organizations (HROs). Trust is fundamental to effective communication, teamwork, and patient care, forming the backbone of reliable and safe healthcare delivery. Through an in-depth case study of a language services program, we explore how gaps in trust and communication can compromise patient safety and care quality. By developing and implementing policy-related instruments, such as a root cause analysis toolkits and emergency protocols, we can address communication barriers and ensure compliance with regulatory standards. By integrating HRO principles with robust policy instruments, healthcare organizations can build and maintain trust, ultimately optimizing patient outcomes and advancing health equity.</jats:p>
