# Architecting Agentic AI Systems for Inclusive and Accessible Library Ecosystems

## Abstract

<jats:p>This chapter per the authors explores how agentic artificial intelligence can re-architect library ecosystems to achieve genuine inclusion and accessibility beyond conventional assistive technologies. The chapter positions agentic AI as a goal-oriented, autonomous, and multimodal partner capable of reasoning, planning, and coordinating tools to respond dynamically to diverse user needs, including those of disabled, neurodivergent, and multilingual patrons. Grounded in digital inclusion theory and social and interactional models of disability, the chapter proposes a comprehensive system architecture comprising unified data layers, reasoning and planning engines, multimodal tool orchestration, and human-agent interaction layers. Applied scenarios illustrate use cases such as accessible wayfinding, cognitive scaffolding, live interpretive communication, cross-lingual research assistance, and staff workflow augmentation.</jats:p>
