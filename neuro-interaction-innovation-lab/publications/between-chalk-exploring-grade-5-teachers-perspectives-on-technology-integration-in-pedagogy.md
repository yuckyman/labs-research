# BETWEEN CHALK: Exploring Grade 5 Teachers' Perspectives on Technology Integration in Pedagogy

## Abstract

<jats:p>This qualitative study employed Braun and Clarke’s (2006, 2019, 2022) six-phase Reflexive Thematic Analysis (RTA) to examine the lived professional experiences of twenty-five (25) Grade 5 public school teachers regarding technology integration in pedagogy. Situated within the Agusan del Sur Division and framed by Mishra and Koehler’s (2006, revised 2019) Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge (TPACK), the Philippine Professional Standards for Teachers (PPST; Department of Education, 2017), and Vygotsky’s (1978) Social Constructivist Theory, the study interrogated how teachers perceive, adapt, and assign meaning to technology use in classrooms characterized by multilingual learners, large class sizes, uneven infrastructure, and evolving post-pandemic curricular demands. Data were collected through semi-structured interviews in Filipino and English, classroom observations, and reflective journals. Following Braun and Clarke’s (2022) updated reflexive protocol, analysis generated five overarching themes: (1) The Philippine Technology Integration Divide; (2) Teacher Identity and Professional Reinvention; (3) School Leadership, DepEd Policy, and the Culture of Compliance; (4) Transformative Technology-Enhanced Learning; and (5) Filipino Teachers’ Aspirations for Technology-Integrated Education. Findings highlight that technology integration in Grade 5 classrooms is a cultural, relational, and structurally mediated phenomenon that extends beyond device provision or technical training. The study advances empirically grounded, teacher-centered, and culturally responsive recommendations for DepEd policymakers, school leaders, teacher education institutions, and practicing educators in the Philippine context.</jats:p>
