# Mn-Driven Ferromagnetism and Electronic Correlations in Multiferroic Bi5Ti3MnO15 from First Principles

## Abstract

<jats:p>We present a comprehensive first-principles study of the structural, electronic, and magnetic properties of the multiferroic Aurivillius phase Bi5Ti3MnO15. Among ten symmetry-inequivalent configurations arising from distinct distributions of Mn3+ across the perovskite B sites, we find a clear energetic preference for inner-site occupancy, consistent with recent experimental observations in related Aurivillius systems. A detailed investigation of the magnetic ground states of these symmetrically inequivalent configurations shows strong ferromagnetic coupling between nearestneighbor Mn3+ cations, driven by superexchange interactions, while interactions between more distant neighbors are significantly weaker. Using Heisenberg exchange parameters extracted from Green’s function calculations, we estimate a Curie temperature for the energetically most stable structure of approximately 300 K within the mean-field approximation. We further demonstrate that the ferromagnetic ground state in these systems is governed by the splitting of Mn t2g and eg orbitals, which is modulated by the Jahn–Teller distortions of the MnO6 octahedra. Explicit inclusion of Hund’s coupling reveals its important role in determining orbital occupation within the Mn-3d orbital manifold, and influencing the competition between ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic interactions. These results establish a unified microscopic picture linking Mn site preference, octahedral distortion, and intraorbital exchange to room-temperature ferromagnetism in Aurivillius multiferroics Bi5Ti3MnO15.</jats:p>
