# Population Health and Health Equity

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>This chapter explores the purpose of population health policy, emphasizing the foundational principle of ethics, and the advance of health equity. It discusses the definition of population health by various group characteristics, such as geographic or demographics factors, and the policies that impact these groups. The chapter critiques non-discrimination laws, which prohibit the exclusion of protected groups but supply no affirmative directive to close gaps in access, affordability, or acceptability of care. It proposes that the true purpose of population health policy is to advance health equity. Drawing on the history of health and human rights onto contemporary standards from accrediting bodies and federal agencies, a unified definition of health equity is presented that emphasizes the ethical commitment to eliminating barriers that prevent individuals from achieving optimal health across all populations.</jats:p>
