# Understanding advanced poly (ethylene glycol)-disulfide-poly (lactic-co-glycolic acid) (PEG-SS-PLGA) nanoparticles for cutting-edge innovations, applications in smart drug delivery systems and targeted cancer therapy

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>Smart drug delivery technologies have become a revolutionary platform in cancer treatment and therapies by enabling precise, stimuli-responsive, and minimum toxic therapeutic interventions. Polyethylene glycol-disulfide-poly (lactic-co-glycolic acid) (PEG-SS-PLGA) have received great interest due to its redox-responsive disulfide functional groups, biodegradability, and ability to self-assemble into nanocarriers with adjustable physicochemical properties. The review gives the overall overview of PEG-SS-PLGA, starting with its chemical structure, synthesis methods, and the major physicochemical properties. It highlights the use of disulfide bond cleavage on redox-triggered in tumour micro-environment with higher levels of glutathione to cause its release into the intracellular environment. This also addresses the nanoparticle formulation methods of nanoparticles, drug encapsulation, kinetics release, in vitro and in vivo performance, as well as the application from monotherapy to co-delivery of chemotherapeutics, siRNA, and immunomodulators. Recent prepreclinical studies provide evidence that has potential to promote therapeutic efficacy, reduces multidrug resistance, and provide theranostic imaging. The review concludes by integrating current knowledge, translational bottlenecks, and recommendations on future directions for optimization them, such as regulatory considerations, preclinical scalability, and incorporation into personalized oncology. Overall, PEG-SS-PLGA represents a promising future platform of targeted, responsive, and multifunctional cancer nanomedicine</jats:p>
