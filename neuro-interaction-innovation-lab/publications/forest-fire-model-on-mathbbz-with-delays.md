# Forest fire model on 
$\mathbb{Z}_+$
 with delays

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>
                    We consider a generalization of the forest fire model on
                    <jats:inline-formula>
                      <jats:alternatives>
                        <jats:inline-graphic xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink" mime-subtype="png" xlink:href="S0021900225100405_inline2.png"/>
                        <jats:tex-math>$\mathbb{Z}_+$</jats:tex-math>
                      </jats:alternatives>
                    </jats:inline-formula>
                    with ignition at zero only, studied by Volkov (2009
                    <jats:italic>ALEA</jats:italic>
                    <jats:bold>6,</jats:bold>
                    399–414). Unlike that model, we allow delays in the spread of the fires and the non-zero burning time of individual ‘trees’. We obtain some general properties for this model, which cover, among others, the phenomenon of an ‘infinite fire’, not present in the original model.
