# MORPHOMETRIC CHARACTERISTICS AND BODY YIELD OF Prochilodus brevis (Steindachner, 1875) ANALYZED BY SEX

## Abstract

<jats:p>Biometric data for Prochilodus brevis body characterization in gonadal development are limited. This study analyzed the morphometric characteristics and body yield of P. brevis by sex in matured individuals. Males-M (n=10, 22.80±0.93 cm/156.50±14.14g) and females-F (n=5, 24.30±2.31 cm/179.67±80.45g) captured and mantained in captivity were submitted to filleting by straight cut of the head and morphometric parameters (length-cm: total-TL, standard-SL, furcal, head and trunk; and height-cm) and body yield (skins, scales, heads, eyeballs, gills, bony skeletons, gonads, gutted tracts and fillets) determined; and gonadal stage by macroscopy and gonadosomatic index (GSI). Although head samples differing (13.00±2.78%-M vs. 9.20±2.26%-F, p&lt;0.05), there was no relationship between morphometry and body yield analyzed by sexes (p&gt;0.05). In gonadal study, macroscopy and GSI (0.02±0.01%-M vs. 0.20±0.01%-F) suggested inverse profile to sex without influencing (p&gt;0.05) skinless fillet (21.15±5.10%-M vs. 18.84±6.52%-F) from SL/TL index (0.78±0.02-M vs. 0.80±0.00-F, p&gt;0.05). Thus, biometric tools not differed in body parameters of matured P. brevis between sex, as contribution to reproductive biology and industrial perspectives.</jats:p>
