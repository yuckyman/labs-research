# ESG e o Agronegócio: Caminhos para um futuro sustentável

## Abstract

<jats:p>This article aims to present the pillars of sustainability, using the history of international multilateral meetings that were dedicated to the study of the environment, seeking greater sustainability in the world. Then, address ESG issues and their relationships with agribusiness. Finally, address issues related to financing the agribusiness sector and best practices respecting ESG requirements, as well as discuss the challenges and opportunities for agribusiness from an ESG perspective. </jats:p>
