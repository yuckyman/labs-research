# Region Making in India’s New Sub-regional Approach

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>India’s contemporary geopolitical momentum has recalibrated the position of the Global South. Commitments over emerging environmental–climate issues along with traditional areas restructured India’s multilateral, neighbourhood and sub-regional negotiations. In this larger transformation, the strategic Northeastern Region (NER) freshly becomes India’s key borderland. Emphasis within the noticeable Neighbourhood First Policy and Act East Policy reproduced the NER in a stable and connected paradigm. The central objective of this chapter is to critically evaluate the new context of the NER and its vigour in India’s sub-regional and neighbourhood engagements. It carefully explores the embedded fault lines of the NER that are mostly drawn from cross-border community relations to potentially make a policy fragile and contentious. The chapter hence proposes a conceptual alternative to reframe the NER as a key Asian landscape in India’s sub-regional approach. It fosters geographical complementarities and shared worldviews in the epistemic logic to augment a durable idea of the NER as a “natural gateway” within the larger debates on sub-regional approaches.</jats:p>
