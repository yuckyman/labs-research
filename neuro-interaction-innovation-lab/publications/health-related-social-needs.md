# Health-Related Social Needs

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>This chapter examines the foundational principle of addressing health-related social needs (HRSNs) in population health policy, underscoring the importance of cross-sectoral collaboration to reduce health disparities. It examines the challenges healthcare organizations face in assessing and addressing social determinants of health, such as housing, through community health needs assessments (CHNAs). The chapter highlights how to better align efforts to address HSRNs between healthcare organizations and community stakeholders, exemplified by a case study on an influenza immunization initiative in Illinois and Wisconsin. This initiative successfully integrated data-driven strategies and community partnerships to address social barriers like medical mistrust and inadequate transportation, demonstrating the effectiveness of aligning healthcare efforts with community needs. By leveraging cross-sectoral collaborations and real-time data, healthcare organizations can enhance their impact on HSRNs, promoting more equitable health outcomes across diverse populations.</jats:p>
