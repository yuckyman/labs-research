# Readiness and Integration of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Tools in Basic Education Schools

## Abstract

<jats:p>Artificial Intelligence (AI) and digital technologies continue to transform educational management and administrative practices in schools. This study examined the readiness, perceptions, and institutional conditions influencing the integration of AI and digital tools in school administration among public schools in San Agustin District, Surigao del Sur, Philippines. The study employed a descriptive research design and utilized a validated researcher-made questionnaire to gather data from 259 respondents composed of teachers, master teachers, administrative personnel, and school heads. Data were analyzed using frequency counts, percentages, weighted means, and one-way ANOVA. The findings revealed that the respondents demonstrated a high level of readiness in terms of digital competence, AI use competence, organizational support, resource mobilization, ethical and responsible AI use, and policy alignment. The respondents also expressed favorable perceptions toward the use of AI and digital tools in school administration and instructional processes. However, the perceived efficiency of the current decision-making process was only moderately efficient, indicating existing challenges related to digital infrastructure, internet connectivity, technological resources, and institutional support. Significant differences in AI readiness were identified among the groups of respondents, particularly in terms of digital competence, AI use competence, organizational support, resource mobilization, and policy alignment.Based on the findings, the study proposed the STRIDE-AI Framework to strengthen AI and digital integration through improved infrastructure, capability-building programs, ethical governance, organizational support, and policy alignment. The framework may serve as a strategic guide for developing a more responsive, efficient, and technology-driven school administration system in educational institutions.</jats:p>
