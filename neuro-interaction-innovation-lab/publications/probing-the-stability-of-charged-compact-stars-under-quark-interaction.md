# Probing the Stability of charged compact stars under quark interaction

## Abstract

<jats:p>Here, the evolution of the structural features and physical properties of the massive compact stars in the framework of f(R,T) gravity under Tolman-Kuchowicz spacetime have been investigated. The presence of anisotropic interacting quark matter at the core of these stars has been considered. Further, we have also investigated the impact of matter-geometry coupling on the mass-radius relationship of the compact stars. The significant role of anisotropy and charge parameter on the spherically stable compact structure under hydrostatic equilibrium has been depicted. These stars have got a more massive structure in f(R,T) gravity as compared with the general relativistic approach. The key role of f(R,T) gravity on the stability of these massive stars becomes very significant under the accelerating expansion of the universe. The obtained results lie in the physically adopted regime which reflects the physical validity of the current model in the context of various astrophysical observations.</jats:p>
