# The Sphere Packing Problem in Dimension Four

## Abstract

<jats:p>We prove that the maximum density of sphere packings in $\mathbb{R}^4$ is $\pi^2/16$, achieved uniquely by the $D_4$ root lattice. The proof establishes that every Voronoi cell in every unit-ball packing satisfies $\mathrm{vol}(V_c)\ge 8$, via a four-layer local argument: shell localisation, root-aligned containment, radial monotonicity, and chamber positivity for $176$ Weyl-orbit types. Non-negativity of the volume defect $F_\Omega=\mathrm{vol}(E_k)-\mathrm{vol}(C_1)\ge0$ is certified at Level~A by an exact rational $LDL^\top$ decomposition (minimum pivot $984/77$) and at Level~B by a Lipschitz sweep with $N=3000$ points and verified margin $0.000088$ (script \texttt{d4\_levelB\_corrected.py}).</jats:p>
