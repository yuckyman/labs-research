# Deep Multinomial Probability Models for Market Segmentation: An Artificial Intelligence Framework

## Abstract

<title>Abstract</title>
                <p>Market segmentation is a fundamental task in modern marketing analytics, enabling organizations to identify distinct customer groups and develop targeted strategies. Traditional segmentation methods often rely on linear multinomial models that may fail to capture complex nonlinear relationships among customer characteristics. This paper proposes a deep multinomial probability framework for market segmentation, integrating neural networks with multinomial probability modelling. The proposed approach estimates customer segment probabilities through a deep learning architecture and provides a flexible alternative to classical multinomial logistic regression. The framework accommodates high-dimensional customer data, nonlinear interactions, and large-scale datasets commonly encountered in modern digital marketing environments. Potential applications, methodological developments, and future research directions are discussed.</p>
