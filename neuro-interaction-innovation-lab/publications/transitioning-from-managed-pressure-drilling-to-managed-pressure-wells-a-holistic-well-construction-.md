# Transitioning from Managed Pressure Drilling to Managed Pressure Wells: A Holistic Well Construction Philosophy in the Mexico Region

## Abstract

<jats:title>Abstract</jats:title>
                  <jats:p>Managed Pressure Drilling (MPD) is widely recognized as an effective technique for drilling wells with narrow pressure windows. However, this paper demonstrates that pressure management should be understood as an integrated well construction philosophy, herein referred to as Managed Pressure Wells (MPW). Rather than treating pressure as a reactive variable, MPW promotes its proactive use as a design, optimization, and control tool throughout the entire well lifecycle, from pre-drilling activities through completion.</jats:p>
                  <jats:p>Field experience documented across multiple onshore and offshore wells in Mexico shows how MPD equipment and principles were successfully applied beyond drilling, enabling operations such as: dynamic and static pore pressure and formation integrity testing (DPPT, SPPT, DFIT, DLOT), controlled mud displacement, trip optimization through swab and surge mitigation, Managed Pressure Cementing (MPC), pressure-controlled coring, cement plug placement without free-fall, reverse casing tie-back cementing, milling of resin plugs and liner couplings in narrow pressure windows, formation logging run on drill pipe, ballooning identification, and flow checks through the MPD system. Additional benefits included early detection and mitigation of losses and influxes.</jats:p>
                  <jats:p>The application of MPW allowed precise characterization of the operational pressure window, reduction of casing strings and hole sections, and simplification of well designs. Documented cases show significant reductions in non-productive time, total well construction duration, and overall costs, while increasing well integrity, operational safety, and decision-making confidence.</jats:p>
                  <jats:p>These results confirm that MPW delivers value beyond risk mitigation. By integrating pressure management across all critical operations, MPW enables smarter well construction, reduces uncertainty, and transforms MPD systems from specialized drilling tools into comprehensive enablers of efficiency, safety, and asset value.</jats:p>
