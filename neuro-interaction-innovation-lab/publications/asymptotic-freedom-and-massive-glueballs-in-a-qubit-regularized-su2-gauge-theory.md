# Asymptotic freedom and massive glueballs in a qubit-regularized SU(2) gauge theory

## Abstract

                      <a:msub>
                        <a:mi>E</a:mi>
                        <a:mn>8</a:mn>
                      </a:msub>
                    </a:math>
                    quantum field theory that can be interpreted as one-dimensional analogs of glueballs. Furthermore, we find

                        <c:mi>σ</c:mi>
                      </c:msqrt>
                      <c:mo>/</c:mo>
                      <c:msub>
                        <c:mi>m</c:mi>

                      </c:msub>

                      <c:mn>1</c:mn>

                    where
                    <g:math xmlns:g="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML" display="inline">
