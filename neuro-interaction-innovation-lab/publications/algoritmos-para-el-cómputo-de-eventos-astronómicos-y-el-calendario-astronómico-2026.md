# Algoritmos para el cómputo de eventos astronómicos y el Calendario Astronómico 2026

## Abstract

<jats:p>We present the results obtained from our algorithms, which were developed through numerical analyses in scientific computing. These algorithms are designed to calculate and predict astronomical events related to specific positions of the Sun, such as solstices and equinoxes; of the Moon, such as supermoons and micromoons; as well as planetary configurations associated with Mercury, Venus, Mars, Júpiter, and Saturn, such as conjunctions, oppositions, elongations, and closest approaches. In addition, some similar events related to the brightest stars near the ecliptic are also included. The calculations were based on data from the US Naval Observatory Astronomical Applications Department regarding the geocentric positions of the celestial bodies for the year 2026. Our Python-based algorithms and computational codes form the backbone of our astronomical calendars, including the 2026 Astronomical Calendar, which is presented in this article. This calendar compiles all the results obtained by our algorithms and adds some others from NASA and the International Meteor Organisation, which are related to eclipses and meteor showers, to enhance its content and fully serve the astronomical community.</jats:p>
