# ANGIKA ABHINAYA IN THE CONTEXT OF BHARATANATYAM NRITYA FORMS

## Abstract

<jats:p>Angika Abhinaya — the expressive use of the body, gestures, and physical movement — forms the cornerstone of Bharatanatyam, a classical South Indian dance form renowned for its aesthetic and narrative depth. While Bharatanatyam’s technical (nritta) and narrative (nritya) components have been well-studied, the systematic analysis of Angika Abhinaya as a semiotic, cognitive, and communicative mechanism remains underdeveloped. This study investigates the structural, performative, and interpretive roles of Angika Abhinaya within Bharatanatyam nritya. Drawing from classical dramaturgical sources (Natya Shastra, Abhinaya Darpana) and contemporary dance scholarship, we employ a qualitative multimethod approach, combining performance analysis of 60 recorded Bharatanatyam pieces, semiotic coding of gestures, and interviews with 15 expert practitioners. The findings reveal that Angika Abhinaya functions not only as an expressive embellishment but also as a primary semiotic system, encoding narrative, emotion, and cultural symbolism. This study contributes to a more nuanced understanding of gesture-based communication in classical dance and underscores Angika Abhinaya’s centrality to narrative and aesthetic comprehension in Bharatanatyam performances.</jats:p>
