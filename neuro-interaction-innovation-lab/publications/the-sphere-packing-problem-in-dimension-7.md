# The Sphere Packing Problem in Dimension 7

## Abstract

<jats:p>We prove that $\Delta_7 = \pi^3/105$, the maximum sphere packing density in $\RR^7$, achieved by the $E_7$ root lattice at unit minimal distance.  The proof applies the Cohn--Elkies linear programming method with an explicit auxiliary function constructed from a weakly holomorphic modular form $G$ of weight $7/2$ for $\Gamma_0(2)$.  The form $G = \theta_{E_7}\cdot(h_2+64)^2/h_2$ carries a double zero at the elliptic point $\tau_0 = (1+i)/2$, non-negative Fourier coefficients, and Atkin--Lehner eigenvalue $-1$; these three properties together force the required sign conditions on the auxiliary function and close the bound.</jats:p>
