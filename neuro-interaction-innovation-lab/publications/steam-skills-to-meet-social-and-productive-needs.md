# STEAM Skills to Meet Social and Productive Needs

## Abstract

<jats:p>Current needs in both industry and academia require highly trained personnel capable of implementing and developing activities that involve the use of modern technologies, enabling efficient responses to everyday problems. Among the key skills required are critical, creative, and computational thinking, as well as problem-solving abilities, collaboration, and interdisciplinary work—competencies that can be effectively developed within a STEAM-oriented educational framework. The present work aims to demonstrate, from a STEAM perspective, how social problems can be adapted and solved according to the demands of a contemporary and highly productive environment. Additionally, a model for the integration of technologies based on a practical example with a specific implementation in engineering programs, through the incorporation of the LSEESC methodology as an example of the use of spreadsheets and active learning sequences in STEAM-oriented education.</jats:p>
