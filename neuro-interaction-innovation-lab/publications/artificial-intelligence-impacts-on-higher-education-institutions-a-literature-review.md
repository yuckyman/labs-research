# Artificial intelligence: impacts on higher education institutions. A literature review

## Abstract

<jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Purpose</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>This study offers an institution-wide and multilevel synthesis of the impacts of artificial intelligence (AI) on higher education institutions (HEIs), explicitly integrating managerial, pedagogical, professional, ethical and emerging cultural-cognitive dimensions, proposing a more holistic analytical framework that captures systemic, behavioral and sociocultural transformations in higher education.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
                  <jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Design/methodology/approach</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>The review adopted an exploratory-descriptive systematic literature review design to identify, classify and synthesize evidence on the strategic, pedagogical, technological and ethical implications of AI implementation in HEIs.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
                  <jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Findings</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>For higher education leaders and policymakers, the findings suggest at least three actionable priorities: (1) the establishment of institutional AI governance frameworks and ethical guidelines; (2) systematic faculty training in AI literacy and academic integrity; and (3) the integration of AI into pedagogical strategies while safeguarding equity, privacy and transparency.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
                  <jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Research limitations/implications</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>The lack of research on its role in the community and its relationship with other HEIs characterizes the limitation of the study.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
                  <jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Practical implications</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>Higher education administrators need to understand the emerging landscape with the advent of AI. Administrators are increasingly required to manage information governance and its implications.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
                  <jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Social implications</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>This new reality offers countless possibilities for the emergence of a more inclusive education, enabling full and disruptive development.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
                  <jats:sec>
                    <jats:title>Originality/value</jats:title>
                    <jats:p>This study offers a multifaceted view of the impacts of AI on HEIs, guiding administrators in managing disruptive environments.</jats:p>
                  </jats:sec>
